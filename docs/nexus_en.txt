[center][size=6][b]Dragonborn's Codex[/b][/size]
[i]An in-game cheat and tool menu for Skyrim SE / AE.[/i]
[i]Everything the console can do and much more, in one window styled after the game's own menus.[/i][/center]

[size=4][b]Getting started[/b][/size]
[list]
[*]Press [b]Insert[/b] to open or close the menu, [b]Esc[/b] closes it. The key can be changed in [i]Service → Menu Hotkey[/i].
[*]Every switch and slider takes effect at once. Nothing to confirm, nothing to restart.
[*][b]Ctrl+F[/b] searches the whole menu, so you don't need to know which page a feature is on.
[*][b]Hotkeys for everything:[/b] click [b]+ Key[/b] next to a feature's name, or right-click the name, then press a key. The hotkey works in the game while the menu is closed. Manage all of them in [i]Service → Hotkeys[/i].
[*]Drag the window by any empty spot, resize it by its edges and corners. Double-click an edge to reset the size.
[/list]

[size=4][b]What's inside[/b][/size]
[list]
[*][b]Home[/b]: your character at a glance, quick toggles, a list of everything that is on right now (each one turns off with a click), recent actions and recently added items.
[*][b]Dragonborn[/b]: god mode, infinite health / stamina / magicka, can't die, regeneration, no weight limit, weightless items by kind, disease and paralysis immunity, night eye, water walking and water breathing, transformations (werewolf, vampire, vampire lord, cures), character profiles (save your character's look and stats to a file and load them onto another character).
[*][b]Combat and movement[/b]: run speed, jump height, size, flight, no clip, free camera, damage dealt and taken, attack speed, one-hit kill, kill moves (with decapitation chances), fast bow draw, [b]aimbot[/b] for bows, crossbows, staffs and spells, slow time while aiming, infinite arrows / staff charge / poison, kill nearby enemies.
[*][b]Stealth[/b]: lockpicking helper, infinite lockpicks, auto unlock, 100% pickpocket, undetectable by AI, hide HUD.
[*][b]Highlights (ESP)[/b]: enemies, NPCs, followers, corpses, containers, items, plants, quest items, doors and levers, traps and [b]unsolved puzzles[/b]. Colours, shapes and labels are yours to set.
[*][b]Magic, shouts, perks[/b]: learn any spell, shout or perk from any mod; free spells, instant casting, no shout or power cooldown; power, duration and cost per school; summons limit; incoming hostile magic.
[*][b]Enchanting[/b]: enchant anything (even items that normally can't be), any effects, any strength, any number of them, your own names; legendary gear.
[*][b]Experience and skills[/b]: level, perk points, attributes, skills, learn everything, unlimited training, auto perks.
[*][b]World and time[/b]: time and weather, stop or slow time, crafting stations anywhere, crafting without perks or materials, infinite ore veins, standing stones, guilds and titles, bounties and the law, followers (immortal, summon, wait here), merchants (rich, restock, buy everything, base prices, summon a merchant), dragons (summon, no dragon attacks), [b]autoloot[/b] and loot nearby, a portable storage chest, [b]solve nearby puzzle[/b] (claw doors, pillars, gears, buttons, levers, chimes, Dwemer resonators...).
[*][b]Teleport[/b]: to any map marker or your own saved points; fast travel from anywhere, in combat and overloaded, without time passing.
[*][b]Quests[/b]: push a stuck quest to its next stage or complete it.
[*][b]Target[/b]: whatever is under your crosshair: kill, resurrect, unlock, claim it as yours, make essential, push away, calm, marry, recruit as a follower, open its inventory, remove from the world.
[*][b]Console[/b]: run console commands with history; save your own command buttons.
[*][b]Add items[/b] and [b]Creatures and NPCs[/b]: every form from every loaded plugin, with search, filter by mod, favourites and recent; kits (smithing materials, all ingredients, filled soul gems, lockpicks, or your own); summon any NPC or creature, go to them or bring them to you.
[*][b]Service[/b]: light (parchment) and dark (gold) themes, compact mode, menu scale, menu hotkey, all hotkeys in one list, language.
[/list]

[size=4][b]Requirements[/b][/size]
[list]
[*][url=https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/30379]SKSE64[/url]
[*][url=https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/32444]Address Library for SKSE Plugins[/url]
[/list]
Tested on Skyrim [b]1.6.1170[/b]. Other game versions are not tested yet.

[size=4][b]Installation[/b][/size]
Install with Mod Organizer 2 or Vortex, or copy the [i]SKSE[/i] folder into [i]Data[/i]. It's a pure SKSE plugin: [b]no .esp, no plugin slot[/b], safe to add to an existing save.
Works with mods: items, spells, perks, NPCs and puzzles from other plugins show up in the menu by themselves.

[size=4][b]Uninstalling[/b][/size]
Turn off what you used and save first, then remove the mod. Values the game keeps in the save itself (for example a changed run speed) stay as they were.

[size=4][b]Languages[/b][/size]
English and Russian. A translation is a single plain .ini file: copy [i]lang\en.ini[/i], translate the values and pick the language in [i]Service → Language[/i]. Translations are welcome.
