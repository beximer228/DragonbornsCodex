# Зачарование page: put an enchantment on an iron sword and a worn iron helmet, take them off; a built-in one (Iron Sword of Fear)
# swaps the item for a plain iron sword. Changes the inventory: reload afterwards.
console coc qasmoke
wait 9
console player.removeitem 12EB7 99
console player.removeitem 89714 99
console player.removeitem 12E4D 99
console player.additem 12EB7 1
console player.additem 89714 1
console player.additem 12E4D 1
console player.equipitem 12E4D
wait 1
# an unknown item makes the bridge refresh the page's inventory snapshot (it is only refreshed while the page is open)
call enchantItem 00000001 0 1
wait 1
call unenchant 00012EB7
wait 1
call enchantItem 00012EB7 0010FB95 55
call enchantItem 00012E4D 0010FB77 50
wait 2
